# Light Random Walks on Graphs

**Status:** placeholder — light treatment only (no MCMC in this course).

## Goals

- Random walk on an undirected graph
- Stationary distribution (degree-proportional)
- Intuition only: mixing / exploration

## Outline

1. One-step transition on an undirected graph
2. Stationary distribution $\pi_v \propto \deg(v)$
3. Why walks explore a graph (short demo)
4. Bridge to SML: Markov chains as a full topic next semester
